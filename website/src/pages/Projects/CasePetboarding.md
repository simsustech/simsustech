## The problem

A boarding or daycare business runs on a paper diary: which dog is in which
kennel, whose vaccination has expired, who still owes for last month. It works
until it does not — a doubled booking, a missed payment, or an afternoon lost to
admin instead of animals.

## What we built

Petboarding, a booking system for boarding and daycare businesses. See it at
[petboarding.app](https://www.petboarding.app) or read the
[product page](/petboarding).

- **Pet profiles** — breed, vaccinations, feeding schedule, medication, weight
  and a photo, searchable in seconds.
- **Daycare operations** — a monthly calendar, prepaid packages, bulk approvals
  and automatic day deduction.
- **A visual kennel layout** where pets are dragged into kennels, with
  vaccination badges, capacity limits and food or medication flags in view.
- **Occupancy and finances** — an occupancy heatmap for capacity planning, plus
  billing that tracks unpaid bookings, invoices and payment history.
- **Three roles** — customer, employee, administrator — each seeing exactly what
  they need.
- **Configurable to the business:** its own pricing, opening times, optional
  services, blocked periods, holiday surcharges and email templates.

## How it is built

A Vue and Quasar front end on a Fastify API — the same stack as our other work.
It is a web application first: it installs as an app, works on desktop, tablet
and mobile, runs in light or dark mode and speaks English or Dutch.

## The result

- The kennel plan is on screen, with capacity limits and vaccination badges
  beside the pet being placed — so a full kennel or an expired vaccination is not
  discovered on the morning it matters.
- Unpaid bookings, invoices and payment history live in the same system that took
  the booking, so billing stops being a separate job.
- Customers arrange a stay without phoning, and the administration is a side
  effect of the booking rather than a task of its own.
