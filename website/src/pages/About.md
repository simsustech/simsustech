## About simsustech
At simsustech we strive to keep technology simple and sustainable. Technology should make life easier, not more complicated.
Some of our core values are:
  - Maintainability
  - Consistency
  - The use of open standards
  - The usage of and attribution to open source software
  - Cooperation
  - Accessibility

> Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.
_Leonardo da Vinci_
