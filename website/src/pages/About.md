## Simple by design

At simsustech we strive to keep technology simple and sustainable. Technology
should make life easier, not more complicated.

That principle decides what we build with. We prefer open standards and
well-supported open-source software, keep interfaces small and predictable, and
avoid dependencies that are hard to replace later.

## Our core values

- **Maintainability** — software you can still change in five years.
- **Consistency** — the same solutions to the same problems.
- **Open standards** — no lock-in to a single vendor.
- **Open source** — we use it, and we credit and contribute back where we can.
- **Cooperation** — we build with you, not just for you.
- **Accessibility** — usable by as many people as possible, by default.

> Simplicity is the ultimate sophistication.

_Leonardo da Vinci_
