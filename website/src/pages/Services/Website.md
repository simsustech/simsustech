## Websites

Built on [Vue](https://vuejs.org/) and [Quasar Framework](https://quasar.dev),
we provide simple but powerful websites for professionals. Your website can be
highly advanced yet still lightning quick thanks to static site generation
(SSG).

### Advantages of Vue and Quasar Framework

- The ecosystem is built around creating high-performance websites with great
  accessibility and loading times.
- With Quasar Framework your website is never limited by the functionality of
  the underlying technology: a simple one-page site can grow into a complete web
  application.
- Both Vue and Quasar Framework offer excellent long-term support.

### Advantages of SSG

- **Performance** — the content is pre-built and served directly to users,
  without server-side processing.
- **Scalability** — serving static files reduces server load and handles large
  volumes of traffic seamlessly.
- **Security** — with no server-side components the attack surface is
  minimised.
- **Cost-effective** — hosting static files is usually cheaper and requires
  less server infrastructure.

## Pricing

Websites start at **€1000** (excl. VAT). There is no fixed price list: what you
pay depends on the number of pages and the features you need. You get a fixed
quote before any work starts.

### What moves the price

- **How many pages**, and how deep the structure goes — a one-pager is a different
  job from twenty pages with sections and subpages.
- **The content.** Copy and images you supply, or we arrange and structure them.
- **Integrations.** A contact form is not a payment provider, a booking system or
  a CRM.
- **A second language.** A translated site doubles the content and doubles what
  has to be checked.
- **The deadline.** A fixed date in a busy month costs more than a comfortable
  one.

### What the quote covers

Structure and design, the build, accessibility and search-engine basics, the
verification run (performance, accessibility, SEO and transfer size) and the
deploy. You own the code and the domain.

After launch, [hosting](/hosting) keeps the site online, monitored, backed up
and updated for **€25 per month**.
