## What we do

simsustech builds and advises on the technology behind a modern web presence.
There are four ways we can help, and [one way of working](/process) that applies
to all of them.

### [Web applications](/webapplication)

Complete applications — front end, API, authentication, payments and database —
on a stack we develop and maintain ourselves.

### [Websites](/website)

Fast, accessible, statically generated websites for professionals, built with
Vue and Quasar Framework.

### [Consulting](/consulting)

Assistance with sustainable energy, mechanical engineering and web or
web-application development.

### [Hosting](/hosting)

Hosting, monitoring, backups and small changes for the sites we build — **€25
per month**, so a site keeps working long after it launches.

### [How we work](/process)

Four steps from first email to handover: a free conversation, a fixed quote,
phases you can stop between, and a handover where you keep the code.

Not sure which one fits? [Get in touch](/contact) and we will work it out
together.
