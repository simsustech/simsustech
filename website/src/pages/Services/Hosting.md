## Hosting

A website is not finished when it goes live. This plan keeps it working, and it
covers the things that actually go wrong in the years afterwards.

**€25 per month** (excl. VAT), per site, on the infrastructure we already run.

### What is included

- **Hosting** on our own infrastructure, with HTTPS that renews itself.
- **Uptime monitoring**, so we hear about a problem before you do.
- **Backups** of the content and the build: a mistake becomes a restore instead
  of a rebuild.
- **Updates**, with every build re-verified on accessibility, SEO and transfer
  size before it ships — the same checks the site passed on the day it launched.
- **Domain and DNS** kept in order.
- **Small changes**: up to one hour per month for text, images, prices and pages
  of the same shape.
- **Support by email**, answered within one working day.

### What is not included

New functionality, a redesign or a new template. Those are quoted as their own
piece of work, which is what keeps this a maintenance plan rather than an
open-ended retainer.

### What €25 per month is worth

€25 is not a floor price, and we do not sell it as one. It is what it costs to
keep a site running without losing money on it.

A hosting package at a Dutch provider costs €4 to €16 per month once the
introductory term ends, and that buys server space only. A maintenance plan for
a CMS site starts around €27 and runs to €70 for a simple site, because
plugins, themes and core updates have to be walked through every month. Our
sites are static — no application server, no database, no CMS to upgrade. That
work does not exist here, which is why this can stay at €25.

It will not get cheaper. Server memory roughly doubled in price over the past
year and hosting prices have risen at nearly every provider. The static design
absorbs that; on a CMS site the same increase lands on the client.
