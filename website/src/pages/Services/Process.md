## How we work

Four steps. You can stop after any of them, and you know the price before the
work begins.

![Four steps: a free conversation, a fixed quote, phases you can stop between, and a handover where the code and domain stay yours](/images/process.svg)

### 1. A conversation, no charge

You describe the problem — a site that loads slowly, a process living in
spreadsheets, a deadline you have to hit. I ask questions until I understand what
"done" means.

You get an honest read on whether it is worth building, whether I am the right
person for it, and roughly what it would cost.

### 2. A fixed quote

I write down what will be built, what it deliberately leaves out, what it costs
and when it is delivered. If it turns out to be more work than that, we agree the
change before it is done — not on the invoice.

You get one page, a fixed price and a date.

### 3. Built in phases

Every phase ends with something you can open in a browser and click through. Each
build is verified before it ships: accessibility, SEO and transfer size are
checked, not assumed.

You get a working build to review, and a short note of what changed.

### 4. Handover

The source code goes into your repository, and the domain and hosting stay in
your name — unless you take [hosting](/hosting) with me.

You get the code, a handover note explaining how to run it, and a plan for
keeping it running.

## What I need from you

- **one decision maker** — the person who can say yes;
- **content**, or the budget to have it written;
- **access** to whatever it has to work with: a CRM, a payment provider, an old
  site or database;
- **a deadline**, if there is a real one.

## How long it takes

The quote states the date, and the phases are sized so you can stop between them.
A small website is measured in weeks; an application in months, because the parts
that surprise you are always in the integrations.
