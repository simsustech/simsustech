## Petboarding

> Easy, fast and reliable pet boarding booking system.

Petboarding is a booking system for pet boarding businesses. It is built for
speed and reliability, so that both the boarding business and its customers can
arrange a stay without friction.

It is built on the same stack as our other work: a Vue and Quasar front end on a
Fastify API.

For more information and pricing, visit
[petboarding.app](https://www.petboarding.app).
